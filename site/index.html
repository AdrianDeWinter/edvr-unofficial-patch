Upscaling and fixes for Elite Dangerous, in VR and flat

ED-UP is a free mod for Elite Dangerous that adds DLSS, FSR and XeSS upscaling and fixes rendering problems, in VR and on a flat screen. In VR it also renders the on-foot game in stereoscopic 3D, where Elite on its own shows a flat 2D projection.

What ED-UP does

Main feature

DLSS, FSR, DittoSR and XeSS upscaling

Elite's own anti-aliasing leaves edges and fine lines shimmering as you move. An upscaler fixes that by blending each frame with the ones before it, and lets the game render below full resolution for a higher frame rate.

  • DLSS on NVIDIA RTX cards
  • FSR 3.1, DittoSR 0.5.0 or XeSS on any GPU
  • Set HMD Image Quality (VR) or Supersampling (flat) below 1.0 to raise the frame rate
  • F7 switches it off and on for a side-by-side look
VRFlat
Main feature

On-foot VR in 3D

On foot, Elite shows VR players a flat 2D projection, with the same image in both eyes. ED-UP renders it in stereoscopic 3D from your character's eyes instead, the way the game already draws the cockpit and the SRV.

  • Your body is there when you look down
  • Aim with your head or the mouse, chosen on first launch
  • Weapon scopes zoom in as they do in flat
  • On by default
VR

Sharpening and HUD anti-aliasing

  • Sharpening (AMD RCAS) for the slight softness upscaling adds
  • HUD anti-aliasing for jagged HUD lines, most visible below 4K
  • Both off by default
VRFlat

Smoke, glare and view fixes

  • Smoke, steam, explosions and solar flares no longer move with your head or ship
  • VRSun glare and night vision stay still as you turn
  • VRNo world lurch when you jump
  • VRNo missing terrain at the edges of view
VRFlat

Frame rate in stations

  • Skips what's outside your hangar while docked with the hatch closed
  • Skips the station outside the concourse on foot; windows show a snapshot until you get close
  • On by default
VRFlat

Visual options

  • Stronger star glow
  • Horizons-style cockpit glass
  • VRWorld scale, bigger or smaller
  • VRHide hyperspace streaks and space dust
  • All start at the game's own look
VRFlat
In-game settingsPress F8 in game to open ED-UP's settings. Most changes apply within about a second, without restarting.
Your filesED-UP doesn't patch Elite's program or data files; its fixes are applied in memory while the game runs. The launcher puts ED-UP's own files beside the game, and where one replaces an existing file, such as Elite's openvr_api.dll, it keeps a backup.
Game updatesIf an Elite update breaks a fix, that fix turns itself off, the rest keep working, and the log says which one.

What the launcher does

01

Keeps ED-UP current

It checks GitHub for new ED-UP releases. Each release is signed with the project's key, and the launcher won't offer one whose signature doesn't check out, so a tampered download can't be installed. Nothing downloads until you press Download.

02

VR, flat or both

It finds the game and installs ED-UP for VR, flat or both. It shows every file it will change and waits for you to confirm, and your settings and other mods keep working.

03

One Play button

Play starts Elite through Steam, Epic or Frontier's launcher. ED-UP's launcher then waits on the taskbar while you play and comes back when the game exits.

First run

  1. Windows may warn that the launcher is unrecognised. The launcher is signed by CharacterEcho LLC, and the warning names that publisher. Windows still warns about a publisher it has little history for, and warns less as that history grows. It is separate from the signature the launcher checks on each update. Choose More info, then Run anyway. To check the file first, compare its SHA-256 with the release's SHA256SUMS.
  2. The launcher installs itself first. The first run copies it to %LOCALAPPDATA%\EDVR\launcher\ and adds a Start menu shortcut. Uninstalling it from Windows' installed apps removes only the launcher and its shortcut; ED-UP stays installed in the game folder, with your settings.
  3. Pick VR and flat, VR only or flat only. Check the list of changes, say yes, and press Play.
